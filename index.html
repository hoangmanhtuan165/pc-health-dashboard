Privacy Policy

PC Health Dashboard · Last updated October 8, 2026

Summary

PC Health Dashboard runs entirely on your computer. It does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information the app reads

To show you the health of your PC, the app reads system information locally, including processor, memory and network usage, drive space and drive health status, battery status, the list of running processes, apps set to start at sign-in, antivirus status reported by Windows Security Center, and whether Windows needs a restart for updates.

This information is displayed on your screen and kept in memory only while the app is running. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Information the app stores

The app saves your preferences (theme and refresh interval) in its private app storage on your PC. Uninstalling the app removes them.

If you choose Export report, the app saves a health report to the location you pick. That file stays on your PC unless you decide to share it.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. Network figures shown in the app are read from Windows' own counters on your PC.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
